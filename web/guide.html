Guide to the map

Add a haunted place, add a note to a mapped place, or correct a record. Each change publishes at once under a name you choose and stays in the place's History.

1Sign in

  1. Choose Sign in at the bottom right, then Don't have an account? Register here.
  2. Enter your email. CUNY addresses work as they are. Other addresses need the guest code from the organizers.
  3. Open the message from ailab@gc.cuny.edu in your usual browser, follow its link, and create a password of 12 characters or more.
  4. Contribute appears beside Account once you are signed in.

Event registration at /register is separate and creates no account.

2Add a place

  1. Choose Contribute, then Edit.
  2. Find the place. Type an address or building and pick a result, paste coordinates or an OpenStreetMap link, or choose Pin and click the map. Points must fall inside the five boroughs.
  3. Title the place and describe what was reported there, in your own words.
  4. Add sources and facts if you have them. Save up to three images if you like; they stay private.
  5. Enter your Public username and Describe your change in a phrase.
  6. Choose Publish, read the preview, and confirm.

The place appears on the map and in Activity at once.

3Add a note to a place

  1. Open Places, find the place, and choose Contribute on its card. Or type the place's name in the form's Place field and pick it.
  2. Write the note. A source or a fact can stand in for a description.
  3. Enter your username, describe the change, and choose Publish.

A note joins the place's record and leaves the place's own words alone.

4Correct a place

  1. Choose Edit on the place card.
  2. Change the fields or sources that are wrong, and explain the change.
  3. Choose Publish.

Each correction publishes as a new version. If the map reports a conflict, reload the place and publish again.

5Name yourself

Use a username or moniker, never an email address. The name appears with your contributions on the map, in History, in Activity and on Contributors. The form remembers it on this device. Leave private information out of the name, the description and the sources.

6Undo, revoke, delete

  • Undo a change from a place's History. The undo publishes a reverse change and leaves later unrelated changes in place.
  • Revoke your own published contribution from Account › Edits. The map and History drop it; your private copy remains.
  • Delete the private copy after you revoke it.

7Find your way

  • Places lists every mapped record. Filters narrow by borough, type, period and certainty. Layers show aerial imagery from 1924 forward.
  • Search, under Tools, finds streets, buildings and coordinates.
  • Tasks lists research prompts to pick up. Activity shows the latest public changes. Contributors counts them by name.
