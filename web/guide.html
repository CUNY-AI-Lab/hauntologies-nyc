Guide to the map

Oct 30, 2026 · @Zach M

Add a place or correct a record. Changes publish at once under your chosen name with changes rendered under Changelog.

1Register and sign in

  1. Choose Sign in, then Register here.
  2. Enter your email. CUNY addresses work as they are. Others need the guest code from the organizers.
  3. Open the message from ailab@gc.cuny.edu, follow its link, and set a password of 12 or more characters.
  4. Contribute appears beside Account.

Event registration on the Register page creates no map account.

2Add a place to the map

  1. Choose Contribute, then Edit.
  2. Find the place. Type an address and pick a result, paste coordinates or an OpenStreetMap link, or choose Pin and click the map. Points must fall inside the five boroughs.
  3. Title the place and describe what was reported there.
  4. Add sources, facts and up to three images you have permission to publish.
  5. Enter your Public username and Describe your change.
  6. Check your entries, then choose Publish.

3Add a note to a mapped location

  1. Choose Contribute, then Edit. Find and select the mapped location in the Place field.
  2. Write the note, a source, or a fact.
  3. Enter your username, describe the change, and choose Publish.

4Address location metadata

  1. Choose Edit on the place card.
  2. Fix the fields or sources and explain the change.
  3. Choose Publish.

5Create a username

Pick a username. It appears with your contributions on the map, in Changelog, Activity and Contributors. The form remembers it on this device. Leave private details out of the name, description and sources.

6Undo, revoke, delete

  • Undo a change from a place's Changelog. Later unrelated changes stay.
  • Revoke your own contribution from Account › Edits. The map drops it. Your private copy stays.
  • Delete the private copy afterward.

7Find your way

  • Places lists every record. Filters narrow by borough, type, period and certainty. Layers show aerial imagery from 1924 on.
  • Search finds streets, buildings and coordinates. On a phone, open Tools to reach it.
  • Tasks lists research prompts. Activity shows recent changes. Contributors counts them by name.
